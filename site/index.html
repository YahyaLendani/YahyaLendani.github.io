SpectraCode

Ask questions about a codebase and get answers that cite the exact file and lines, with every citation checked against the source. YL-HAILCAST*HAILCAST*

Hailcast

Real-time data engineering and demand forecasting for taxi trips. A replayed event stream runs through a Bronze, Silver and Gold pipeline into LightGBM forecasts and a live FastAPI dashboard. A collector stitches about 2,500 live NYC bus positions from MTA Bus Time into trips and plays them back on a map. It runs on its own Oracle Cloud VM with Docker Compose, PostgreSQL and Caddy; CI tests and boots the image on every push, and a written security review backs the public demo. The taxi data is synthetic, and in a test planned before scoring, LightGBM did not beat repeating the same hour from the day before.

YL-LEAD-SNIPER*LEAD-SNIPER*

Lead Sniper

An autonomous 24/7 lead-monitoring and application bot running on an Oracle Cloud VM. It polls twelve freelance and job feeds (Freelancer.com, NoFluffJobs, JustJoin.it, Bulldogjob, GitHub Bounties, HackerNews and six remote boards) over async httpx with SQLite WAL deduplication. Postings pass keyword scoring and a fast Groq LLM engineering-fit check before the bot drafts CV-grounded proposals, places daily-capped bids through Freelancer's official API, emails public hiring inboxes with a PDF resume attached, and sends private Discord DM receipts plus approve, edit and skip buttons. Forms behind CAPTCHAs stay manual and route straight to Discord.

YL-CLINICAL-ML*CLINICAL-ML*

Heart Disease Clinical ML

Can a heart-disease model be trusted at the bedside? A Graph-Augmented Clinical Transformer (PLR periodic embeddings and UniMP masked neighbor attention) tested alongside classical baselines and GNNs on 68,589 OpenML screening patients, 1,221 angiography patients across five hospitals (Cleveland, Budapest, Zurich, Long Beach VA and Tehran) and 4,172 prospective Framingham Heart Study patients. Packaged as a zero-pickle, sub-10ms 2-stage FastAPI and HL7 FHIR R4 decision-support prototype: Stage-1 screening reaches 0.8007 ROC-AUC (0.0094 ECE, +0.1232 over European ESC SCORE2 Poland 2021 and +0.0991 over Framingham 2008), and deferred cases escalate to 5-center Stage-2 cardiology workup at 0.9212 ROC-AUC (0.9322 PR-AUC, 0.8% sex sensitivity gap). Zero-label weighted conformal calibration lifts prospective Framingham coverage +12.0 pts, while 2-second local site recalibration slashes Framingham ECE by 98.6% (0.4233 to 0.0061, restoring 90% coverage from 66.8% to 90.9%), backed by 132 tests and mypy in CI, silent-mode shadow-trial tooling, Prometheus and Grafana monitoring, and a 9-slide defense deck. Retrospective research prototype only; mortality prediction on the 275-admission MIMIC-IV demo remains a pipeline demonstration.

Contents
Python, PyTorch, XGBoost, FastAPI, HL7 FHIR R4, Docker
